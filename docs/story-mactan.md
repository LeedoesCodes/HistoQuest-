# Arc 1 — Labanan sa Mactan (The Battle of Mactan), 1521

**Status:** DRAFT for review. Not yet converted to game content.
**Audience:** Grade 5 (about 10–11 years old)
**POV:** The pupil plays a *fictional ally* — a young Mactan seafarer — never Lapu-Lapu himself.

> ⚠️ **Read the "Historical notes" section at the bottom before approving.** Some
> details below are well documented; a few are contested or invented for drama and
> are flagged. Your adviser or an Araling Panlipunan teacher should sign off.

---

## 1. Design principles for this arc

| Principle | How it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Hook in the first 10 seconds** | Open mid-tension (sails already on the horizon), not with a history lecture. |
| **The child is useful, not a bystander** | The ally's *local knowledge of the reef* is what matters — something a kid can feel proud of. |
| **Personal stakes before national stakes** | A younger sibling and a fishing boat come before "the nation." Abstractions don't move 10-year-olds. |
| **The clever underdog** | The teachable core: Mactan won by using shallow water, not by being stronger. Brains over armour. |
| **Choices shape experience, not history** | Per the manuscript, every path converges on the real outcome. |
| **Respect, not caricature** | Magellan is determined and mistaken, not a cartoon villain. Humabon chose differently, not treacherously. |

**Reading level:** short sentences, concrete images, active voice. Aim ~8–14 words per sentence.

## 2. Character roster

Used for the cinematic character-intro cards.

### Lapu-Lapu — *Datu of Mactan*
- **Who:** The leader of Mactan. Older, calm, watchful.
- **Wants:** To keep Mactan free and its people unharmed.
- **Fears:** That his people will be made to kneel to someone who has never fed them.
- **Line that defines him:** *"Hindi kami tumatanggi sa panauhin. Tumatanggi kami sa panginoon."*
  ("We do not refuse a guest. We refuse a master.")

### Ikaw / You — *Batang mandaragat (young seafarer)*
- **Who:** A child of Mactan who fishes the shallows every day. Unnamed, so any pupil can be them.
- **Wants:** To be treated as useful, not sent away with the little ones.
- **Fears:** That being small means being helpless.
- **Superpower:** Knows exactly where the coral is — where a heavy boat will run aground.

### Ferdinand Magellan — *Kapitan ng mga dayuhan*
- **Who:** A determined captain far from home, commanding ships for the King of Spain.
- **Wants:** To claim these islands, spread his faith, and prove the world can be crossed.
- **Blind spot:** He believes armour and guns settle everything. He never studies the water.
- **Line:** *"Ang hindi yuyuko sa hari, yuyuko sa espada."*
  ("Whoever will not bow to the king will bow to the sword.")

### Rajah Humabon — *Pinuno ng Cebu*
- **Who:** Leader of nearby Cebu who made peace with the strangers and was baptised.
- **Wants:** Safety and trade for Cebu.
- **Why he matters:** He shows children there was *a real choice*, and reasonable people chose differently.

### Amihan — *Nakababatang kapatid (younger sibling)* — **invented character**
- **Who:** The ally's small sibling. Appears twice; gives the danger a face.

## 4. Script

Format per beat: **FIL** (primary) then **EN** (translation).

---

### M1 — Sails at dawn · `story`

**FIL**
> Madilim pa nang ginising ka ng katahimikan.
> Walang ibon. Walang alon sa bahura.
> Tumakbo ka sa dalampasigan — at doon mo sila nakita.
> Mga layag. Malalaki. Hindi galing dito.

**EN**
> It was still dark when the silence woke you.
> No birds. No waves on the reef.
> You ran to the shore — and there they were.
> Sails. Huge ones. Not from here.

---

### M2 — Who you are · `character` (Ikaw)

**FIL**
> Ikaw ang batang mandaragat ng Mactan.
> Araw-araw kang nangingisda sa mababaw na tubig.
> Kabisado mo kung saan matalim ang bato. Kung saan sumasadsad ang bangka.
> Walang mapa ang mga dayuhan. Ikaw ang mapa.

**EN**
> You are a young seafarer of Mactan.
> Every day you fish in the shallow water.
> You know where the coral is sharp. Where a boat runs aground.
> The strangers have no map. You are the map.

---

### M3 — Lapu-Lapu · `character`

**FIL**
> Dumating si Lapu-Lapu, ang datu ng Mactan.
> Hindi siya sumigaw. Tumingin lang siya sa dagat, matagal.
> "Ilang taon kong binantayan ang tubig na ito," sabi niya.
> "Hindi ko ipauubaya sa hindi kilala ang lasa ng asin nito."

**EN**
> Lapu-Lapu came, the datu of Mactan.
> He did not shout. He only looked at the sea, for a long time.
> "I have guarded this water for years," he said.
> "I will not hand it to someone who has never tasted its salt."

---

### M4 — Why they came · `story`

**FIL**
> Malayo ang pinanggalingan nila — mga buwan ang layo.
> Naghahanap sila ng pampalasa: klabo, kanela, paminta.
> Sa kanilang lupain, kasinghalaga ito ng ginto.
> Ngunit hindi lang pampalasa ang hinahanap nila.
> Gusto rin nilang tawaging kanila ang mga pulo — at ang mga tao rito.

**EN**
> They came from far away — months of sailing.
> They were looking for spices: cloves, cinnamon, pepper.
> In their land, those were worth as much as gold.
> But spices were not all they wanted.
> They also wanted to call these islands theirs — and the people in them.

---

### M5 — Magellan · `character`

**FIL**
> Ferdinand Magellan ang pangalan ng kanilang kapitan.
> Matapang siya. Hindi siya sumusuko.
> Naglakbay siya nang malayo kaysa kaninuman noong panahong iyon.
> Ngunit may hindi siya pinag-aralan: ang tubig.
> Akala niya, sapat na ang baluti at baril.

**EN**
> Ferdinand Magellan was the name of their captain.
> He was brave. He did not give up.
> He had sailed farther than almost anyone alive.
> But there was one thing he never studied: the water.
> He believed armour and guns were enough.

---

### M6 — Humabon's choice · `story`

**FIL**
> Sa kabilang pulo, sa Cebu, may pinuno — si Rajah Humabon.
> Nakipagkasundo siya sa mga dayuhan. Nagpabinyag siya.
> Para sa kaniya, kaligtasan at kalakalan ang tama.
> Hindi siya duwag. Iba lang ang napili niyang daan.
> Sa Mactan, iba ang pasya.

**EN**
> On the other island, in Cebu, there was a leader — Rajah Humabon.
> He made peace with the strangers. He was baptised.
> For him, safety and trade were the right choice.
> He was not a coward. He simply chose a different road.
> In Mactan, the answer was different.

---

### M7 — The demand · `story`

**FIL**
> Dumating ang mensahe sa Mactan.
> Magbigay ng pagkain. Magbayad ng buwis. Kilalanin ang hari sa malayo.
> Tumayo si Lapu-Lapu sa harap ng mga tao.
> "Hindi kami tumatanggi sa panauhin," sabi niya.
> "Tumatanggi kami sa panginoon."
> Ramdam mo ang kilabot sa iyong batok. Wala nang aatras.

**EN**
> The message reached Mactan.
> Give food. Pay tribute. Bow to a king across the sea.
> Lapu-Lapu stood before his people.
> "We do not refuse a guest," he said.
> "We refuse a master."
> You felt a chill on your neck. No one was turning back now.

---

### M8 — The plan · `story` ← **teachable core**

**FIL**
> Ngunit hindi lakas ang plano ni Lapu-Lapu. Talino.
> "Mabigat ang kanilang barko," sabi niya. "Mababaw ang ating tubig."
> "Hindi sila makakalapit. Kailangan nilang maglakad sa dagat."
> Napatingin siya sa iyo.
> "Batang mandaragat — kabisado mo ang bahura, hindi ba?"
> Tumango ka. Sa unang pagkakataon, hindi ka masyadong maliit.

**EN**
> But Lapu-Lapu's plan was not strength. It was cleverness.
> "Their ships are heavy," he said. "Our water is shallow."
> "They cannot come close. They will have to walk through the sea."
> He looked at you.
> "Young seafarer — you know the reef, don't you?"
> You nodded. For the first time, you were not too small.

---

### M9 — How will you help? · `decision` (8s)

**Prompt — FIL:** *Paano mo tutulungan si Lapu-Lapu?*
**Prompt — EN:** *How will you help Lapu-Lapu?*

| id | FIL | EN |
|---|---|---|
| `reef` | Ituro ang mababaw na bahura | Show them the shallow reef |
| `warn` | Ilikas si Amihan at ang mga bata | Get Amihan and the children to safety |
| `spears` | Ihanda ang mga sibat sa dalampasigan | Ready the spears on the shore |

All three converge on M10. Each sets a one-line flavour response:

- `reef` — **FIL:** "Ituro mo sa amin ang daan nila." / **EN:** "Show us the road they must take."
- `warn` — **FIL:** "Mabuti. Walang batang masasaktan ngayon." / **EN:** "Good. No child will be hurt today."
- `spears` — **FIL:** "Maghanda. Malapit na sila." / **EN:** "Get ready. They are close."

### M11 — The tide turns · `story`

**FIL**
> Nangyari ang sinabi ni Lapu-Lapu.
> Sumadsad ang malalaking bangka sa bato.
> Kinailangang lumusong ng mga kawal — mabigat ang baluti, malalim ang putik.
> Ang kanilang kanyon ay masyadong malayo para makatulong.
> Mabagal sila. Kayo ay hindi.

**EN**
> What Lapu-Lapu said came true.
> The big boats struck the coral.
> The soldiers had to wade in — heavy armour, deep mud.
> Their cannons were too far away to help.
> They were slow. You were not.

---

### M12 — After the battle · `story`

**FIL**
> Hindi nakalapag ang mga dayuhan.
> Sa mababaw na tubig ng Mactan, natalo ang kanilang hukbo.
> Doon din nasawi si Magellan.
> Bumalik ang mga natitira sa kanilang barko, at umalis.
>
> Si Lapu-Lapu ang naging unang pinunong Pilipino na matagumpay
> na lumaban sa mga dayuhang mananakop.
> Hindi dahil mas marami sila. Kundi dahil kilala nila ang sarili nilang dagat.
>
> At ikaw — ang batang kabisado ang bahura — naroon ka.

**EN**
> The strangers never landed.
> In the shallow water of Mactan, their force was beaten.
> Magellan fell there too.
> The rest went back to their ships, and sailed away.
>
> Lapu-Lapu became the first Filipino leader to successfully
> resist foreign invaders.
> Not because they had more men. Because they knew their own sea.
>
> And you — the child who knew the reef — you were there.

## 5. Historical notes (verify before approval)

**Well documented — safe to keep**
- Date: 27 April 1521, Mactan. Magellan died in the battle.
- Magellan's expedition sailed for the Spanish crown, seeking the spice route.
- Rajah Humabon of Cebu allied with Magellan and was baptised.
- The Spanish ships **could not get close because of shallow water/coral**, so their cannons could not support the landing party and the men had to wade a long distance. This is the tactical heart of the arc and is supported by Antonio Pigafetta's eyewitness chronicle.
- Magellan's landing party was small (a few dozen) against a far larger Mactan force.

**⚠️ Contested or uncertain — check with your adviser**
1. **Did Lapu-Lapu personally kill Magellan?** The arc deliberately does **not** claim this. Pigafetta describes Magellan being overwhelmed by many warriors, not slain by Lapu-Lapu personally. Popular retellings often assert it; I'd keep our neutral wording.
2. **Exact troop numbers.** Pigafetta gives figures (often cited as ~1,500 Mactan warriors vs ~49 Spaniards) but historians treat these cautiously. The script avoids numbers on purpose.
3. **Lapu-Lapu's personal details** — birth year, religion, even the spelling of his name — are genuinely uncertain. The script gives him character but no biography.
4. **All dialogue is invented.** No quotations from Lapu-Lapu or Magellan survive. If your panel expects it, add an on-screen note that conversations are dramatised.

**Invented for the story (clearly fictional, per the manuscript's fictional-ally design)**
- The player character (young seafarer) and their reef knowledge.
- **Amihan**, the younger sibling.
- The idea that a child guided the defenders to the reef.

## 6. Open questions for Lee

1. **Should the ally have a name?** Currently unnamed ("ikaw") so any pupil projects onto them. A name adds warmth but reduces identification.
2. **Keep Amihan?** She adds emotional stakes but is one more invented element to defend.
3. **Is Magellan's portrayal acceptable?** I wrote him as determined and mistaken rather than evil. Some teachers prefer a firmer framing of colonisation.
4. **Length:** 12 beats ≈ 4–6 minutes of reading for a Grade 5 pupil, plus the mini-game. Too long, about right, or too short?
5. **Do you want an on-screen "dramatised dialogue" disclaimer** at the start of each arc?
